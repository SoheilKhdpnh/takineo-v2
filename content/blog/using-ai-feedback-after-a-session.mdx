After a speaking session, a transcript and a list of “errors” can look authoritative. They are not a lesson plan.

Work through AI notes in this order:

1. Keep examples that came from **your** sentences.
2. Drop suggestions that sound unlike you, or that you would never say.
3. Ask the teacher which two items are worth practising before the next 15 minutes.

The goal is a short, personal loop: speak, review, speak again. AI can speed up the middle step. It should not invent a syllabus you did not earn in conversation.

If the notes and the teacher disagree, trust the teacher. That is the product.
